Pettify Privacy Policy

We respect your privacy and your pets' data.

Last updated: November 17, 2025

Overview

This Privacy Policy describes how Pettify ("we", "our", or "the app") handles information when you use our mobile application. We are committed to protecting your personal information and being transparent about how we use it.

By using Pettify, you agree to the collection and use of information as described in this policy.

Information We Collect

Pettify collects the following types of information:

  • Pet profiles — name, species, breed, date of birth, weight, photos, and notes you enter
  • Health records — veterinary visits, medications, vaccinations, and medical documents you upload
  • Memories & photos — images and captions you add to your pet's memory timeline
  • Reminders — reminder titles, dates, and times you configure for pet care tasks
  • AI chat messages — questions and messages you send to the in-app AI assistant
  • Backup data — if you use the backup feature, an export of your app data
  • Device & usage information — advertising identifier, app usage patterns (via Google AdMob, for ad personalisation)

Local Storage

All your pet data — profiles, health records, memories, and reminders — is stored locally on your device using an encrypted SQLite database. We do not upload this data to any server unless you explicitly use the backup or export features.

🔒 Your pet's data stays on your device. Pettify does not have access to your personal pet records.

AI Assistant

Pettify includes an AI-powered pet assistant. When you send a message to the assistant, your query is sent to third-party AI providers to generate a response. These queries may include text you type about your pet's situation or health.

We do not store your AI conversations on our own servers. However, the AI providers we use may process and retain messages in accordance with their own privacy policies.

App Permissions

  • Photo Library — to let you add pet photos and memories. Only accessed when you choose a photo.
  • Notifications — to send you reminders for vet appointments, medications, and care tasks. You can disable these at any time in device settings.
  • Files / Documents — to allow you to import documents (e.g. vet reports) and export PDFs. Files are accessed only when you interact with these features.
  • Internet access — required for ads and AI assistant features.

Advertising

Pettify uses Google AdMob to show ads in the free version of the app. AdMob may collect and use your advertising identifier (IDFA on iOS, Advertising ID on Android) to deliver personalised advertisements.

On iOS, we request App Tracking Transparency (ATT) permission before accessing your IDFA. You can opt out of personalised ads at any time through your device settings.

Upgrading to Pettify Premium removes all ads from the app.

Third-Party Services

Pettify integrates with the following third-party services:

Google AdMob In-app advertising platform. Collects advertising identifiers for ad personalisation. policies.google.com/privacy
AI Language Model Providers Process AI assistant queries. Messages are sent to generate responses; not stored by Pettify. See individual provider policies for data retention details.
Expo / React Native App framework. May collect crash reports and diagnostics to improve app stability. expo.dev/privacy

Data Retention & Deletion

Since your data is stored locally on your device, you are in full control of its retention. You can delete individual pets, records, photos, or memories directly within the app. Uninstalling Pettify will remove all locally stored data from your device.

If you have created backups, you are responsible for managing and deleting those backup files from wherever you saved them (e.g. your Files app or cloud storage).

Children's Privacy

Pettify is not directed at children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us and we will take steps to delete such information.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically. Continued use of Pettify after any changes constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
